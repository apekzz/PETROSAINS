2. OPENCLIP MASKED-CROP CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------
Method:
- Ground-truth segmentation polygons were used to isolate embedding quality
  from YOLO mask errors.
- Each object was tightly cropped with all non-object pixels set to black.
- OpenCLIP ViT-B-32 generated normalized 512-dimensional embeddings.
- Queries were matched against 4,355 training catalog embeddings from 109
  classes.
- Final ranking replicated inventory_app: top 100 neighbors, mean similarity
  per class with at least three supporting embeddings, and rejection at <=60%.
